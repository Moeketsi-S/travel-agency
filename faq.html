Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question about our products, orders or custom cakes? Find answers to some of our most frequently asked questions below.

General Questions

Where is Sweet Crumbs Bakery located?

Sweet Crumbs Bakery is based in Johannesburg, South Africa. Visit our Contact page for more information about our location and how to get in touch with us.

What products do you offer?

We offer a variety of freshly baked products including celebration cakes, cupcakes, pastries, bread and cookies.

Do you make custom cakes?

Yes. We create personalised cakes for birthdays, weddings, graduations, anniversaries, baby showers and other special occasions.

Orders

How do I place an order?

You can browse our products online and contact Sweet Crumbs Bakery to discuss your order. For custom cakes, you can complete our custom cake enquiry form.

Request a Custom Cake

How far in advance should I place my order?

We recommend placing custom cake and large orders as early as possible. The required notice period may vary depending on the size and complexity of the order.

Can I place a last-minute order?

Last-minute orders may be possible depending on our availability and the type of product required. Contact us as soon as possible and we will try to assist you.

Can I change my order after submitting it?

Order changes may be possible if the bakery has not yet started preparing the order. Please contact us as soon as possible if you need to make a change.

Custom Cakes

What information do you need for a custom cake?

We ask customers to provide information such as the event date, preferred flavour, cake size, theme, colours, approximate budget and any specific design requirements.

Can I provide my own cake design?

Yes. Customers can provide design ideas or reference images when discussing their custom cake requirements. Final designs will depend on what can be realistically produced by our bakery team.

How much do custom cakes cost?

Custom cake prices vary depending on factors such as size, flavour, ingredients, decorations and design complexity. Customers will receive a quotation based on their requirements.

Do you make themed cakes?

Yes. We can create cakes based on a variety of themes, colours, interests and celebrations.

Collection and Delivery

Can I collect my order?

Yes. Customers can arrange to collect their orders from Sweet Crumbs Bakery at the agreed collection time.

Do you offer delivery?

Delivery availability may depend on the order size, delivery location and scheduling. Please contact us to discuss delivery options.

What happens if I am late for collection?

Please contact the bakery as soon as possible if you are unable to arrive at the agreed collection time so that alternative arrangements can be discussed.

Payments

What payment methods do you accept?

Available payment methods will be confirmed when your order is placed. Customers should confirm payment requirements with the bakery before finalising their order.

Do I need to pay a deposit for a custom cake?

A deposit may be required to confirm certain custom orders. The amount and payment requirements will be communicated when the order is confirmed.

Ingredients and Allergies

Can I ask about ingredients?

Yes. Customers are encouraged to contact us if they have questions about the ingredients used in a particular product.

Do you cater for dietary requirements?

Some dietary requirements may be accommodated depending on the product and ingredients required. Please contact us before placing an order to discuss your requirements.

What if I have a food allergy?

Customers with allergies should inform us before ordering. Because multiple products may be prepared in the same bakery environment, customers should discuss their requirements with our team before placing an order.

Still Have Questions?

If you cannot find the answer to your question here, our team will be happy to assist you.
